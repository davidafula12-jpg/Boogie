                Lending and returning

                In the catalogue, select Lend book and pick the student. On the Loans page, use Return book, Renew (extends the due date) or Lost (for lost or damaged books).

                Students who leave

                Archive them. They disappear from lists but their borrowing history is kept. Archived students can be restored.

                Teachers and staff

                Add them with the type Teacher / staff. They have their own limits under Settings.

                End of term
